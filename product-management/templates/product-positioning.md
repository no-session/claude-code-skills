# Product Positioning

How we position ourselves in the market — our narrative, differentiation, and competitive stance. This document is the single source of truth for how we talk about our product externally.

---

## Positioning Statement

Use April Dunford's positioning framework:

> For **[target customer]** who **[key need/struggle]**,
> **[Product]** is a **[market category]**
> that **[primary benefit]**.
> Unlike **[primary alternative]**,
> we **[key differentiator]**.

### Example:
> For engineering teams who struggle to ship reliably at scale,
> [Product] is a developer platform
> that turns chaotic release cycles into predictable, confident deployments.
> Unlike legacy CI/CD tools,
> we combine deployment automation with real-time production intelligence.

### Tone
- **Confident, not arrogant.** We state what we believe clearly without putting others down.
- **Specific, not vague.** Numbers, examples, and concrete language over buzzwords.
- **Human, not corporate.** Write like a smart colleague explaining something, not a press release.
