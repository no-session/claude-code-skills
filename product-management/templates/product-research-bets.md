# Product Research Bets

Our current research areas and strategic bets — where we're investing in learning and exploration beyond our current roadmap.

---

## What Are Research Bets?

Research bets are areas where we're investing time and resources to explore opportunities that aren't yet validated enough for the main roadmap. They sit between "wild idea" and "committed initiative."

The goal of a research bet is to **reduce uncertainty** — to learn enough to either promote the bet to a committed initiative or kill it with confidence.

## Research Bet Lifecycle

```
Idea → Proposal → Active Research → Decision → Promote / Kill / Pivot
```

### Stage 1: Idea
Anyone on the team can propose a research bet. Write a 1-paragraph thesis: "If we build X, we believe Y will happen because Z."

### Stage 2: Proposal
The PM or sponsor fills out the bet template above and presents it at the quarterly planning review. Leadership decides whether to allocate resources.

### Stage 3: Active Research
The assigned team explores the bet within the allocated time and resources. This might include:
- **Customer discovery interviews** (5-10 conversations focused on the problem space)
- **Technical spikes** (Can we build this? What are the hard parts?)
- **Prototype/MVP** (Build the smallest thing that lets us test the thesis)
- **Market analysis** (What do competitors do here? What's the market size?)
- **Data analysis** (What does our existing data tell us about this opportunity?)

### Stage 4: Decision
At the end of the time horizon, the team presents findings and recommends one of:

| Decision | Meaning | What Happens Next |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| **Promote** | Strong evidence that this is worth committing to | Becomes a committed initiative in the next cycle. PM writes a full PRD. |
| **Kill** | Evidence suggests this won't work or isn't worth pursuing | Document what we learned. Archive the bet. Celebrate the learning. |
| **Pivot** | The original thesis was wrong but we found something adjacent | Rewrite the thesis and continue researching with a new direction. |
| **Extend** | Promising but we need more time/data | Allocate additional resources for one more research period. Max 1 extension. |

## Guardrails

1. **Total research investment should be ~15% of capacity.** Enough to learn, not so much that it starves committed work.
2. **Maximum 3 active bets at a time.** Focus beats breadth.
3. **Every bet has a time box.** No open-ended research. If we can't learn enough in the allocated time, that itself is signal.
4. **Killed bets are celebrated.** Learning what NOT to build is as valuable as learning what to build. We never punish teams for killing a bet.
5. **No zombie bets.** If a bet has been "paused" for more than one quarter, it's dead. Kill it formally and free up the mental space.
