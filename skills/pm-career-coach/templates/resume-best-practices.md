# PM Resume Best Practices

## Resume Structure (Top to Bottom)

### 1. Contact Information (2 lines max)
Name, location (city, state), email, LinkedIn URL. Phone number optional. For AI PM roles, add GitHub and portfolio links.

### 2. Professional Summary (3-5 lines)
Who you are + what you do + one major accomplishment + target role direction. Skip entirely if under 3 years of PM experience.

**Formula:** "[Level] Product Manager with [X] years of experience in [domain]. [Biggest achievement with metric]. [What you're looking for or where you're headed]."

**Example:** "Senior Product Manager with 6 years in B2B SaaS growth. Led the self-serve monetization strategy at Acme that grew ARR from $8M to $22M in 18 months. Seeking a product leadership role at a Series B-C company where I can own a P&L."

### 3. Work Experience (Reverse Chronological)
- Most recent 2 roles: 5-7 bullets each
- Earlier roles: 3-5 bullets each
- Max 2 lines per bullet
- Roles older than 10 years: 1-2 lines or omit

### 4. Projects Section (For Career Changers)
Side projects that demonstrate PM thinking. Shrinks as professional experience grows. Include: problem identified, approach taken, outcome achieved.

### 5. Skills Section
Technical skills, tools, and methodologies. Strongest/most relevant first. Include: analytics tools, prototyping tools, technical skills, methodologies (Agile, Jobs-to-be-Done, etc.).

### 6. Education
Degrees and relevant certifications. Minimal space if experienced. MBA or CS degrees are relevant; include GPA only if 3.5+ and under 5 years out.

## Formatting Rules

- **Length:** 1 page under 8 years, 2 pages max for 8+
- **Margins:** 1/2 inch to maximize content
- **Layout:** Single-column, top-to-bottom. No tables, graphics, sidebars
- **Fonts:** Arial or Calibri, 10-11pt body, 12-14pt name
- **File format:** PDF unless ATS specifically requests .docx

## Writing Bullet Points

### The XYZ Method (Google's Recommended Format)

**"Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"**

| Component | What It Covers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| X (Accomplishment) | What you achieved | "Launched enterprise SSO integration" |
| Y (Measurement) | How you know it worked | "closing 12 blocked deals worth $2.4M ARR" |
| Z (Method) | How you did it | "by partnering with security eng to build OAuth2 flow in 6 weeks" |

### Action Verbs by PM Activity

**Strategy & Vision:** Defined, Envisioned, Pioneered, Championed, Established
**Execution & Shipping:** Launched, Shipped, Deployed, Delivered, Released
**Growth & Optimization:** Grew, Increased, Improved, Optimized, Accelerated
**Analysis & Discovery:** Identified, Discovered, Validated, Analyzed, Uncovered
**Leadership & Influence:** Led, Aligned, Negotiated, Persuaded, Drove
**Innovation:** Introduced, Created, Designed, Architected, Invented

**Verbs to avoid:** Managed, Assisted, Helped, Supported, Participated, Contributed, Responsible for, Involved in

### Metrics to Include

**Growth metrics:** MAU/DAU increase, user acquisition, activation rate, conversion rate
**Revenue metrics:** ARR/MRR growth, deal size, LTV increase, revenue per user
**Efficiency metrics:** Time-to-value reduction, onboarding completion rate, support ticket reduction
**Engagement metrics:** Retention rate, NPS improvement, feature adoption, session duration
**Delivery metrics:** Time-to-market, sprint velocity, release frequency

**Rule:** Pair input metrics (what you built) with output metrics (what happened). "Shipped X" alone is incomplete. "Shipped X, resulting in Y" is what hiring managers want.

### Before/After Examples

**Bad:** "Managed the product roadmap and worked with engineering to deliver features on time."

**Good:** "Defined Q3 roadmap prioritizing self-serve onboarding, shipping 4 features that improved Day-1 activation from 34% to 58% and reduced support tickets by 40%."

---

**Bad:** "Conducted user research and identified pain points in the checkout flow."

**Good:** "Ran 30 user interviews uncovering a 3-step friction point in checkout, then shipped a simplified flow that lifted conversion by 12% ($1.8M annual revenue impact)."

---

**Bad:** "Led cross-functional team to launch new product."

**Good:** "Led eng (6), design (2), and data science (1) to launch predictive churn scoring from 0→1, acquiring 40 enterprise customers in first quarter ($800K ARR)."

---

**Bad:** "Responsible for growth initiatives across the platform."

**Good:** "Owned acquisition funnel optimization: redesigned landing pages, launched referral program, and built in-product upgrade prompts — growing paid conversions by 28% QoQ."

## Resume for Career Changers

### Hybrid Format
Lead with a "Relevant Experience & Projects" section before chronological work history. Include:

1. **Side projects** that demonstrate full product lifecycle thinking
2. **Transferable accomplishments** from current role, reframed with PM language
3. **PM coursework or certifications** (Product School, Reforge, etc.)

### Reframing Non-PM Experience

| Current Role | PM-Relevant Reframe |
|---|---|
| Engineer | "Identified user pain point in [feature], proposed solution to PM, and shipped fix that reduced error rate by X%" |
| Designer | "Led discovery for [feature], conducting X user interviews and A/B testing Y variants, resulting in Z% improvement" |
| Marketing | "Defined positioning for [product launch], aligning go-to-market with product roadmap and driving X signups" |
| Consultant | "Diagnosed client's [problem], built business case for [solution], and managed implementation delivering $X impact" |
| Support/CS | "Identified top 5 user pain points from 500+ tickets, built internal dashboard tracking resolution, influenced roadmap prioritization" |

### What to Include for First PM Role
- **Career objective:** 2 lines explaining the transition and what you bring
- **Portfolio link:** Required. Even 2-3 case studies showing PM thinking
- **Side project:** Build something — even a product teardown or redesign proposal
- **Relevant skills from current role:** Data analysis, stakeholder management, user empathy, technical depth

## AI PM Resume Specifics (2026 Market)

AI PMs earn a 15-20% premium. To position as an AI PM:

- List AI-specific skills: prompt engineering, model evaluation, RAG architectures, vector databases, fine-tuning, AI safety/alignment
- Integrate technical skills into accomplishments: "Deployed personalized recommendations using [framework] on [platform], increasing conversion by X%"
- Show you understand probabilistic systems: mention model accuracy, hallucination rates, evaluation metrics
- All AI experience must be hands-on — no theoretical padding
- Include GitHub/portfolio links showing AI project work
